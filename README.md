# Belonging Conditions Practice

A private, repeat-use reflection and planning practice for educators, coaches, leaders, and learning teams.

## Experience

- Frames belonging as the developing experience of being recognized, able to enter and contribute, and expected to matter—without becoming less oneself.
- Opens with a concise What If / Because / Then proposition and an optional “Why belonging?” primer.
- Guides a Notice → Interpret → Tend → Return cycle without turning people or conditions into scores.
- Places **Belonging Is the Compass** between interpretation and action as an optional orientation song for the adult tending the field—not as a classroom practice—and gives it projected lyrics, a song download, a clean lyric sheet, a separate practice guide, three adult-use frames, and a next-noticing prompt.
- Supports attention to one learner, partners or a small group, a whole class, an adult team, or another context.
- Includes eight belonging signals and 21 practices across relationship, participation, identity and language, environment, and music.
- Gives every practice a rationale, practical entry, belonging guardrail, and signal to notice afterward.
- Turns the playable **I Am Me**, **Every Voice Belongs**, and **Brighter Than Before** moments into complete in-context SongBridge rooms: intentional player, projected lyrics, song downloads, clean lyric sheets, separate practice guides, three grounded use cases, and a belonging-specific “Notice next” prompt. Playback is always chosen by the visitor; nothing autoplays.
- Uses one steady, full-screen lyric window across all four songs. Lyrics advance phrase by phrase with the recording rather than drifting continuously, hold their place when paused, and remain freely scrollable for preview or reflection. Song-specific vocal-entry timing keeps instrumental openings from carrying the words past their entrance; the single play/pause control keeps attention on the lyrics rather than the player.
- Supports field-created practices when the best move is already known by the person closest to the setting.
- Turns a selected move into a time-bound tending cycle with a possibility goal, rhythm, return date, and fidelity guardrail.
- Automatically preserves the current draft and allows an observation to be saved before a practice is chosen.
- Provides a My Entries field journal for resuming developing notes, reopening prior entries, and reflecting on active cycles.
- Exports the full local archive as JSON and copies a concise carry-forward plan.

## Pattern View

Pattern View makes repeated experience visible without treating recurrence as diagnosis. It surfaces:

- condition trajectory;
- signal recurrence;
- practice-and-next-observation pairings;
- tending repertoire by ecological category;
- categories and distinct moves explored or not yet explored;
- open questions; and
- a gentle next-noticing invitation.

Concentration is not assumed to be a problem. A highly concentrated repertoire may fit the context; the interface simply makes it available for reflection.

## Visual system

The responsive constellation is both atmosphere and functional trace. Selected signals illuminate nodes, related lines gather strength, and connected signal pairs become more visible. Custom line symbols and a restrained motion system distinguish Belonging while keeping it in the same authored family as Agency Conditions Practice. Body text remains sans-serif and comfortably sized; serif type is reserved for large display headings.

## Agency cross-pollination

Belonging’s Pattern View suggests a strong next layer for Agency Conditions Practice: signal recurrence, agency-state trajectory, practice-and-response pairings, repertoire concentration, unexplored entryways, and next-noticing prompts. These should be adapted to Agency’s own logic rather than copied mechanically.

## Privacy

No account or shared database is used. Drafts and saved reflections remain in browser local storage unless the user copies a plan or exports the archive. The archive preserves the earlier `songbridgeBelongingArchiveV02` storage key for continuity when this version replaces an earlier build on the same browser origin.

## Files

Serve the `dist` directory as a static website. `index.html`, `style.css`, `song-data.js`, `app.js`, `audio`, and `downloads` must remain together. The audio folder contains three classroom-practice recordings plus the adult-facing **Belonging Is the Compass** orientation recording. The downloads folder contains one clean lyric sheet and one educator-facing practice guide for each song.
